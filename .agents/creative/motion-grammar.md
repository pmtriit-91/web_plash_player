# Motion Grammar

Use for motion design, scroll storytelling, 3D-like UI, GSAP, Framer Motion, React Three Fiber.

## Motion philosophy

Motion must have a reason:

- Guide the eye.
- Create depth.
- Give the user a sense of discovery.
- Explain relationships between elements.
- Create an emotional rhythm.

## Avoid cheap motion

Avoid:

- Overusing repetitive fade-ups.
- Meaningless scale/bounce.
- Excessive neon flicker.
- Unordered concurrent animations.
- Overpowering hover glow.

## Interaction choreography

When clicking a hotspot:

1. Hotspot glows softly.
2. Camera/perspective shifts.
3. Background dims or blurs slightly.
4. Detail/part highlights.
5. Panel appears last.
6. Metrics animate with stagger.

## Scroll storytelling

- Use sticky/pinned sections for cinematic transitions.
- Avoid meaningless black gaps between scenes.
- The next scene must evolve from the previous one, no hard cuts.
- Use progressive reveal instead of showing everything.
