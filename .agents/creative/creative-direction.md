# Creative Direction

Use for tasks related to landing pages, brand sites, premium UI, cinematic experiences, product showcases.

## Goal

The agent must not only code correctly but also create a tasteful experience.

Priorities:

- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Plenty of intentional breathing space.
- Less but impactful.
- Do not abuse cards/grids/templates.
- Avoid overly mechanical UI.
- Do not show too much information at once.
- Evoke emotion first, provide information later.

## When designing premium UI

Always ask silently:

- What is the user's main focal point?
- What emotion does this section evoke?
- Does the motion guide the eye?
- Is the layout too uniform and mechanical?
- Should the UI chrome be reduced to make the product stand out more?

## Do not

- Do not turn everything into a dashboard.
- Do not use random animations.
- Do not create multiple boxes/cards unless necessary.
- Do not overuse glow effects.
- Do not cram text into hero/product scenes.
