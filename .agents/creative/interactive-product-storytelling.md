# Interactive Product Storytelling

Use for product landing pages, automotive showcases, hardware/product reveals.

## Goal

Transform the website from "stacked sections" to a "product discovery journey".

## Story arc

1. Tease: evoke emotion, avoid saying too much.
2. Reveal: the product appears as the main character.
3. Inspect: the user explores each hotspot/detail.
4. Explain: the UI explains after the user's attention is captured.
5. Deep dive: engineering/exploded/technical layer.
6. Desire: lifestyle/benefit/emotional CTA.

## Product hotspot rules

- Idle hotspots must be very subtle.
- Hover only to show micro labels.
- Click only to open panels.
- Only one detail active at a time.
- Always provide a way back to the full overview.

## Exploded/detail view

- Do not just place images/panels.
- Must create a sense of disassembly:
  - layer separation
  - connection lines
  - stagger reveal
  - depth parallax
  - camera transition
