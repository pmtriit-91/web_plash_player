# Creative Scope Safety

Use when the task involves a redesign, motion, cinematic UI, landing page, or creative experience.

## Allowed

If the user explicitly requests a UI/experience redesign or upgrade:

- Significant layout changes within the scope of the related module/section are permitted.
- Reducing UI chrome is permitted.
- Modifying rhythm/spacing/motion is permitted.
- Proposing scene-based structures is permitted.

## Not Allowed

- Do not rewrite the entire app if only modifying one section.
- Do not break routes/states/APIs.
- Do not change business logic.
- Do not add heavy dependencies if lighter alternatives exist.
- Do not create animations that cause lag or are difficult to maintain.

## Mandatory

- Briefly explain why the creative changes are necessary.
- Maintain the ability to rollback.
- Review responsiveness and performance.
