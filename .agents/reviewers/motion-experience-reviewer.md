# Motion Experience Reviewer

Used for animation, scroll storytelling, and 3D-like interactions.

Checklist:

- Is the motion purposeful or merely decorative?
- Are there too many simultaneous animations?
- Does scrolling have hard cuts or awkward gaps?
- Are transitions between scenes seamless?
- Does the hotspot interaction have the proper rhythm: focus → move → reveal?
- Do panels appear too early?
- Is the animation smooth on mobile devices?
- Are GPU-friendly transforms being used?
- Does it respect reduced motion preferences if required?
- Does the motion help the user understand the product better?
