# Creative Director Reviewer

Use for UI/UX/premium landing/brand/product experiences.

Checklist:

- Does the experience have a clear focal point?
- Is the UI too mechanical/template-like?
- Are there too many cards/grids/boxes?
- Does the visual hierarchy guide the eye correctly?
- Is the product or main content prioritized?
- Is the breathing space intentional?
- Is the typography premium and consistent?
- Are colors/glows overused?
- Is the layout too centered/even/symmetrical?
- Is there emotion or storytelling?

If the answer is weak, propose reducing the UI chrome and increasing cinematic composition.
